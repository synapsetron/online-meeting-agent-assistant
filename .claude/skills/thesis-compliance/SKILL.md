# Thesis writing and compliance

Use the approved thesis assignment as the source of project-specific requirements. If it is missing, mark requirements as unverified and do not invent them. Write in academic Ukrainian unless asked otherwise; retain original API, standard, and publication names. Distinguish project design, sourced claims, assumptions, and measured results.

Trace every assignment task to a method or implementation, evidence/artifact, and conclusion. For formulas define symbols, domains, units, assumptions, and parameter estimation. For algorithms specify inputs, outputs, steps, and complexity where relevant. Cover the required topics only when supported by the assignment and repository evidence: MAS roles and coordination, agenda analysis, Meet integration, LLM/API implementation, and accuracy/performance evaluation.

Cite only checked sources; do not invent references, measurements, novelty, or completed work. Verify current official standards and department guidelines before claiming formatting compliance. List unresolved requirements explicitly and distinguish “specified,” “implemented,” “measured,” and “needs confirmation.”

Useful source: [Anthropic Agent Skills format](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/overview). Confirm institutional formatting rules against current official department/standards materials.
